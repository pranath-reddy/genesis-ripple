Simulation-design reference
---------------------------

The bounded simulation specification records the SLSim tutorial repository at
revision cbf2165f35c29278b255e4b679d51c84cf86e5d1 as design evidence for the
single-lens and false-positive examples. Tutorial files are not distributed in
this repository and are not a runtime dependency.
